Ryan Reynolds timeline

  • 1991: Made his television acting debut in the teen drama series Fifteen(also notes as Hillside)
  • 1993: Made his feature film debut in Ordinary Magic and appeared in the television series The Odyssey.
  • 2002–2007: Was in a high-profile relationship with singer Alanis Morissette; the pair were engaged from 2004 until they split in 2007.
  • 2004: Starred in the action film Blade: Trinity as Hannibal King, showcasing a major physical transformation.
  • 2008–2011: Married actress Scarlett Johansson before separating in late 2010 and finalizing their divorce in July 2011.
  • 2011: Starred as Hal Jordan in the superhero film Green Lantern, where he met his future wife
  • 2012 (September 9, 2012): Married Blake Lively in a private ceremony in South Carolina.
  • 2014 (December 16, 2014): Welcomed their first child, daughter James.
  • 2016: Released the massive box-office hit Deadpool, which he also produced and championed for years; they also welcomed their second daughter, Inez, in September 2016.
  • 2018: Acquired an ownership stake in Aviation American Gin and later led Mint Mobile as a prominent owner/spokesperson.
